Majors, earnings and jobs

Does your major actually pay off?

Ask about any college major. A team of research agents queries real US earnings, employment and graduate-survey data in parallel, then writes a short answer where every number is cited.

What it explores

Pay, gender gaps, unemployment, earnings growth after graduation and typical jobs for bachelor's majors, plus what graduates themselves report: whether they work in their field and how satisfied they are with their job.

Who it's for

Students, families and advisors who want evidence, not folklore, before choosing a major. US data.

How to use it

Ask a specific question, or tell it your priorities. Vague questions get a clarifying question first.
